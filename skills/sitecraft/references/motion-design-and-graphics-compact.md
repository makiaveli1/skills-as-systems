# Compact motion design and motion graphics route

Use for kinetic type, animated identity, composited artwork, interactive vectors, or motion-led web storytelling. Load `motion-design-and-graphics.md` for full detail.

## Brief

**Interface motion** explains state or navigation. **Motion graphics** are authored sequences with beats, type, compositing, and media delivery. Hybrid projects keep separate budgets.

Record job, emotion, trigger, lifecycle, interruption, stable end state, storyboard, style frames, animatic, protected assets, responsive variants, reduced/static equivalent, runtime budget, and evidence. Remove purposeless motion.

Choose motion depth: Quiet, Polished, Chapter-led, or Motion-led. For chapter-led work, load `narrative-motion-and-living-media.md`. Define arrival, transition, readable hold, handoff, living-media behaviour, and fallbacks. Derive the continuity device from the brief; never require one recurring motif or reuse a reference site's signature scene grammar.

## Grammar

Use about 70–120 ms for direct feedback, 140–260 ms local, 240–450 ms structural, and 350–700 ms for rare expressive beats. Define enter, exit, standard, emphasised, and direct-manipulation easing. Keep repeated actions quiet, stagger only for hierarchy, and finish with content stable.

## Route

Choose the lightest route:

- CSS for simple state/decorative motion;
- Web Animations for controlled DOM sequences;
- View Transitions for progressive continuity;
- SVG for shape, mask, icon, or simple type motion;
- Lottie/dotLottie for supported vector sequences;
- Rive for interactive vector state machines;
- canvas/WebGL/WebGPU for dense rendering or 3D;
- video for photorealistic or heavily composited sequences.

Record format, runtime/version, features, rights, editable master, fallback, owner, and removal path. Never choose a runtime before the brief.

## Safety and evidence

Kinetic type needs reading order, readable holds, responsive re-composition, and a semantic or transcript equivalent. Compositing needs alpha, masks/mattes, layer order, perspective, light, edges, motion blur, colour space, crop, and edge checks.

Respect reduced motion. Replace non-essential travel, parallax, zoom, depth, loops, and peripheral motion. Automatic motion over five seconds needs pause, stop, or hide unless essential. Never exceed three flashes per second unless below WCAG thresholds. Provide skip, poster, captions, transcript, or semantic equivalents. Preserve keyboard, focus, reading order, and status.

Keep LCP simple, reserve space, stop hidden/offscreen/settled playback, cap concurrent motion, and measure frame pacing, INP, CPU, GPU, memory, requests, and constrained devices.

Require storyboard, style frames, animatic, lineage, screenshots, and wide, short, narrow, and reduced-motion recordings. Test input, cancellation, failure, fallback, and performance.

A still cannot approve motion. A recording cannot prove accessibility or performance. AI output remains a candidate until tested and approved.
